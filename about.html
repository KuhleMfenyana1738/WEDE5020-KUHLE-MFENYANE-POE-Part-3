SJ Holdings and Logistics (Pty) Ltd is a South African-based logistics and transportation company providing efficient, safe, and cost-effective freight and supply chain solutions across the country and neighboring regions. We specialize in moving goods with precision, speed, and accountability while supporting businesses with tailored logistics services that improve operational performance. Our mission is to simplify logistics for our clients by offering dependable transportation, warehousing, and distribution services backed by technology, skilled professionals, and strong operational systems. SJ Holdings and Logistics Company stands at the forefront of modern logistics in South Africa — committed to innovation, excellence, and sustainable growth. Our Vision and Mission reflect a dynamic enterprise equipped to navigate future industry challenges while empowering clients and communities alike.

Vision Statement

To be South Africa’s leading integrated logistics partner — redefining excellence in supply chain solutions that empower businesses to grow sustainably, compete globally, and deliver value to every community we serve. Expand network operations across Africa, enabling seamless connectivity between regional markets and international trade corridors.

Mission Statement

SJ Holdings and Logistics Company exists to deliver tailored, end-to-end logistics and supply chain solutions through operational excellence, strategic innovation, and deep customer focus — creating sustainable value for clients, employees, communities, and stakeholders across South Africa and Africa.
